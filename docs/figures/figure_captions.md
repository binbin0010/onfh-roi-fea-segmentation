# Manuscript Figure Captions

## Figure X. Anatomy-adaptive ONFH ROI workflow

Workflow of the V3 research segmentation module for expert-reviewed
osteonecrosis of the femoral head (ONFH) ROI definition and downstream finite
element analysis (FEA) preprocessing. The workflow integrates femoral-head
sphere fitting, subchondral distance mapping, anterosuperior weight-bearing
priors, patient-adaptive cancellous-bone intensity thresholds, seeded region
growing, morphology constraints, automatic quality control, and expert review
before STL/JSON/CSV export. The experimental collapse-feature score is provided
only as a research descriptor and does not represent a validated collapse
probability, ARCO stage, or treatment threshold.

## Figure Y. Schematic comparison of PVIBGT and SHD-IBG

Schematic comparison of pedicled vascularized iliac bone graft transfer
(PVIBGT) and surgical dislocation with impaction bone grafting (SHD-IBG) for
hip-preserving treatment of ONFH. PVIBGT preserves the hip in reduction and
transfers a vascularized iliac flap with its pedicle to the debrided femoral
head defect. SHD-IBG uses greater-trochanteric osteotomy and surgical hip
dislocation to expose the femoral head, remove necrotic bone, and pack
cancellous autograft into the defect. The drawing is a conceptual manuscript
schematic and is not intended as an operative instruction.
